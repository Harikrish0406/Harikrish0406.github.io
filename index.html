RIL KPI Builder

Automated earnings parser and KPI builder for Reliance Industries — built for an ITUS Capital assignment. Fetches 15 years of quarterly filings directly from BSE's public API, extracts financials from native-text and scanned/OCR PDFs, applies the required comparability adjustments (excise deduction, GST discontinuity, segment merger, capex handling), and outputs a full KPI sheet with an adjustment log and data-quality notes. Includes a drift-watchdog layer that flags new or disappearing disclosure fields across quarters. 83% exact match against a hand-built reference sheet on directly comparable metrics, with remaining gaps traced to OCR reading-order issues on old scanned filings and documented honestly rather than hidden.

NSE Quant Signal Stack

A layered signal-validation and portfolio-construction pipeline for NSE equities, rebuilt after a code audit found a lookahead bug in the highest-weighted signal of an earlier version and hardcoded (not measured) weights elsewhere. Every signal here earns its weight in the final decision from its own leak-free, walk-forward-validated performance — a signal that fails validation is excluded entirely, not just down-weighted. Includes a purpose-built leak-free validation framework, an XGBoost/LightGBM ensemble, and a backtest of the combiner itself, not just its ingredient signals. Shared here as an architecture/methodology showcase — live validation results and trading data are intentionally excluded.

MNC Job Scraper

Pulls live job openings directly from company career-site APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday's public CXS endpoint) rather than relying on manual search or guessed URLs — every company/platform pairing in the registry is verified by a direct API hit before being trusted. Filters for entry-level/graduate roles in Chennai or India-remote, with a location-bucketing pass that catches postings vaguely labeled "Remote" but actually geo-restricted outside India. Includes a discovery helper that finds a company's real Workday tenant/site from its public careers page rather than blind-guessing tenant strings.
